# Roamly Groups · Positioning Framework & Statement, Module 3 (Exercise 1)

## Responses
- **FOR:** The organiser of a friend-group milestone trip, four to ten people, already a Roamly user. Not group travellers in general, and not the participants. The one person who volunteered and is now carrying it.
- **WHO WANT / NEED:** To book one experience for the whole group without fronting the money and chasing friends to get it back. The problem is not splitting a bill. It is being personally exposed to people they care about for money they have already spent.
- **WE OFFER:** A way to book a curated local experience for a whole group where the booking, the money and the guest list are one thing. Everyone joins with a tap, pays their share, and the booking confirms once the group has.
- **BECAUSE OF:** The organiser stops being the bank. They get to be on the trip instead of running it.
- **DESPITE:** Roamly is not where trips start. 40 cities against competitors' hundreds. No tools for the deciding phase. And it has never held a group's money before, which is a real trust ask, made harder because Airbnb tried and abandoned it.
- **BETTER THAN:** Airbnb, where the booking confirms against one card and the platform never knows the group exists. And the WhatsApp stack, which settles money after the booking, which is exactly when it fails.
